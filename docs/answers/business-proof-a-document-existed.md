# How can a small business prove an invoice, contract or design existed on a date?

*PROOF · updated 2026-10-25*

> **Short answer.** Make a proof of the exact file at the moment it matters: when you send the invoice, sign the contract, share the design or publish the policy. A proof fingerprints the file and has the fingerprint time stamped by independent witnesses, so later you can show that this exact version existed by that date and has not changed. An email's sent date helps but is controlled by the mail provider and can be disputed, and a file's own date can be edited in seconds. Prove whole folders at once for things like month end invoices or a project handover. Keep the original files with their proofs. A proof shows when a version existed, not who wrote it or that the other side received it.

## Where dates get argued about

Late payment on an invoice that the client says arrived late. A contract where each side has a slightly different version. A logo or website design a client later says they made themselves. A refund policy a customer says was different when they bought. In each case the question is the same: which version existed, and when.

For many small businesses the cost of a dispute is the time it takes, not the amount. A proof made on the day turns a long argument about dates into a short one about facts.

## What a proof does not do

It does not show who wrote the document, that the other side received it, or that a court will accept it. It is one strong fact among the others you keep: emails, delivery receipts, signed copies. Many courts, including in India, already accept electronic records with a certificate about how they were produced, and a proof's facts help with that.

## A habit for the business

1. **Prove at the moment it matters.** Sending an invoice or quote, signing a contract, handing over a design, publishing a policy.
2. **Prove folders, not just files.** Month end invoices, a project handover, a product photo shoot: one proof covers them all.
3. **Keep the originals.** A file saved again, printed and scanned, or sent through a chat app no longer matches.
4. **Store file and proof together.** In the same folder, backed up the same way.
