# How do I prove what was on my laptop before giving it for repair?

*PROOF · updated 2026-10-18*

> **Short answer.** Prove your important files before the laptop leaves, and keep a sealed record while it is away. A proof made the day you hand it over shows exactly what your files were at that moment: if anything is changed or planted later, it no longer matches. A record kept during the repair shows what was opened, copied or plugged in while the laptop was out of your hands. Together they answer the two questions people ask afterwards: was my data like this before, and what did they touch? Back up first, too: a proof shows what a file was, it does not bring back a file that is lost.

## Why both

A proof of your files settles what they were. It cannot say who opened them. A record of the repair says what happened, but not what the files were beforehand. With both, a dispute stops being your word against theirs.

## The morning you hand it over

1. **Back up.** Copy everything important off the laptop and check the copy opens.
2. **Prove your important folders.** Documents, photos, anything you would hate to see changed or copied.
3. **Start a record of the repair.** So what happens while it is away is written down and sealed.
4. **When it comes back.** Read the record, and check your files against their proofs.
