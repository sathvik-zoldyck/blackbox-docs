# About Alcyone Secure

*A company named after a star. A product named after a judge.*

Alcyone Secure builds **Rashnova**, the evidence layer that keeps an honest record of what happens
on your laptop. Both names were chosen for what they mean, and this page is the story of both.
Our thesis has not changed: security is not just prevention. **Security is accountability.**

> Rashnova was called **Black Box** until 2026. It is the same recorder from the same team, with a
> new name and the same promises.

## The names

### Alcyone, the brightest star of the Pleiades

The Pleiades sit on the shoulder of Taurus, a small cluster of stars that people have used to mark
the seasons for thousands of years. In Persian they are called *Parvin*. Alcyone is the brightest
of them. We named the company after it because a bright, fixed point is what you need when you are
trying to find your way back to what actually happened. The owl in our mark is the watcher that
sees in the dark; the star above it is Alcyone.

### Persia sealed every record, so nobody could quietly change it

Between 509 and 457 BCE, the administrators of Persepolis wrote every movement of grain, wine and
flour on a small tablet of clay. Each tablet recorded a single transaction, and each one was rolled
with a seal, so anyone could see who had authorised it and that nobody had altered it since. More
than fifteen thousand of those records survive. We know how the empire ran, day by day, because
somebody kept an honest record and sealed it.

Rashnova does the same thing for your laptop: one entry for every event, each one sealed to the
one before it.

### Rashnu weighed every deed, and could not be deceived

In the Zoroastrian tradition of Persia, Rashnu is one of the three judges of souls, called "the
straightest". At the bridge between worlds he weighs a life on golden scales that favour no one:
not the rich, not the powerful, not the person being judged.

> "As much as a hair's breadth it will not turn."
> *Mēnōg-ī Xrad 2.121, translated by E. W. West, 1885*

The tradition weighs three things: good thoughts, good words and good deeds. Rashnova keeps only
one of them. It never reads your thoughts (what is inside your files) or your words (what you
type). It keeps an honest record of deeds: what was done on the machine, and when.

**Rashnova** (rash · NO · vuh): Rashnu, the judge who could not be deceived, and *nova*, a star
that suddenly shines bright, a new light beside Alcyone. Together: an honest record of what
actually happened, whoever did it.

### A recorder is orange so it can be found

Aviation's flight recorder is painted international signal orange, so investigators can find it in
wreckage. It is engineered to survive the crash, the fire and the ocean floor, because its only job
is to make sure the truth survives the event. It does not prevent the crash; it makes the truth
recoverable, and that single property helped turn aviation from the most dangerous way to travel
into the safest. We borrowed the colour and the job description. Not prevention. Evidence.

## Five constraints every feature has to pass

These are engineering constraints, not marketing values. Anything that cannot pass all five is not
built, however good it would look on a roadmap.

| | Constraint | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Local first** | Activity stays on the device by default. You stay in control. |
| 02 | **Private by design** | Every entry is encrypted on your device before anything leaves it. |
| 03 | **Tamper evident** | Alteration is detectable and provable. |
| 04 | **Works offline** | No continuous connection is needed. |
| 05 | **Deliberately small** | One job, done well, on parts the industry has trusted for decades. |

## A note from the founder

Anyone who has handed a laptop to a repair shop knows the feeling. The machine that holds your
photos, your money and your work is in a stranger's hands, behind a counter you can't see past. And
every security product you own has gone quiet, because none of them were built for this.

Antivirus looks for malware. It was never built to notice a person. The technician browsing your
files, the colleague at your unlocked desk, the intern with day one access to everything: they
don't break in. They are handed the keys, and they look exactly like normal work.

Rashnova is our answer. Not prevention: a witness. A recorder that writes down what happens to your
device in a form nobody can quietly rewrite, encrypted so that nobody, including us, can read it
but you. We are building it deliberately small and deliberately honest: local first, sealed,
provable when it matters.

Trust is good. Proof is better.

**Sathvik R**, Founder, Alcyone Secure

## The paid version came second. On purpose.

**You could have built the paid company product first. Why didn't you?**
Because Rashnova was never a startup idea. It started with tracking channels that trafficked stolen
private photographs, and an operator who named repair shops as a source: technicians copying
customer drives before repairs. The customers never knew. So the product that didn't exist had to
exist first.

**Then where does the money come from?**
Companies. The recorder is free forever; that is not a launch offer, it is the point. The cloud
layer is funded the way GitHub is: businesses pay for fleet features, and their money holds the
price for individuals at zero, or close enough not to matter. Until then, development is paid for
by our DPDP compliance audit practice, not by your data.

## What we will never do

Each of these has one acceptable value, and it is zero. The list can be added to, never shortened.
If a number ever moves, you hear it from us first, publicly.

| We will never | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Read your files | 0 |
| Hold a key that opens your logs | 0 |
| Sell or share what the recorder sees | 0 |
| Put ads anywhere in the product | 0 |
| Send anything home without being asked | 0 |

We also do not partner with repair businesses, and we will not: the product works for the owner of
a device, not for whoever is temporarily holding it.
